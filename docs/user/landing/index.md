# Presto AI Dash

## Always in view. Never in your way.

Monitor AI usage, subscription limits, reset times, and service health across your accounts—all at a glance on a dedicated display.

**No windows. No screen clutter. Just the information you need.**

Presto AI Dash currently targets the **Pimoroni Presto** touchscreen. It requires a separate computer or server running the Python daemon or Docker collector; the Presto connects over Wi-Fi. Additional display hardware is being explored but is not yet supported.
